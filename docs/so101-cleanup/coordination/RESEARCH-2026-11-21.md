## Closest adjacent work, clearly separated from hackathons

- [so101-claude](https://github.com/omjambhale/so101-claude): physical foam-letter arrangement with front/wrist cameras and MCP actions. A persistent camera process, pixel tools, IK and a four-point table calibration provide a very close interface precedent. [Demo publication commit](https://github.com/omjambhale/so101-claude/commit/b38ed59b461bcd3c9a9cdce525f22c6b8ad9f87b) is dated 19 November; recording date, hackathon participation and repeatability are unverified. Its advertised arbitrary tasks are not all demonstrated.
- [SO-101 typing](https://github.com/machinezhou/so101_typing): dated hardware milestones on 29–30 September. A local 2×2 command-to-image map, bounded corrections and fresh-frame convergence checks address fine alignment. The report explicitly separates commanded displacement from measured tool displacement and discusses dead zone/backlash. Its supervised descent configuration is not suitable to copy into autonomous cleanup.
- [RoboHarness V2 workspace design](https://github.com/BinceQu/RoboHarness/blob/main/harness/v2/README.md), [release chronology](https://github.com/BinceQu/RoboHarness#news): V2 released 25 October, further results 5 November. ## Changes recommended for our staged plan

These are engineering inferences from the evidence, not source claims about our robot:

1. **Keep semantic reasoning and physical execution separate.** One persistent Station owner supplies the same operations to CLI and MCP. The next demo uses our proven deterministic actions; collecting a policy-training dataset is deferred.
2. **Give every stage an observable postcondition.** Distinguish a completed motor command from a held object or completed delivery. Use held / empty / uncertain assessments, and allow already-satisfied stages to be skipped only with fresh evidence.
3. **Use both views for verification.** After a short lift, look for paper held by the gripper and a cleared source patch. Neither signal alone proves success. Reobserve ambiguity before carrying; verify release separately. Thin wrappers make gripper aperture especially weak evidence.
4. **Learn local correction directions through small measured experiments.** Fit a local image-displacement map at a checked hover, validate separately, and use bounded corrections with fresh observations. Diagnose dead zone and commanded-versus-observed motion before increasing steps. This does not supply table height or whole-table grounding.
5. **Make recovery resumable and inspectable.** Reacquire after a miss, change the failed grasp hypothesis, retry only from a checked checkpoint, and share a per-object attempt budget. Save images and structured events for offline replay.
6. **Measure the demonstration honestly.** Report all started trials, including aborts, first-grasp success, success after recovery, false success assessments and cycle time. Separate paper from wrapper results; five trials are an initial diagnostic pilot.

See the [updated implementation plan](../workstreams/WS-2026-11-21-dynamic-loop.md). First slice remains persistent tools, dual-camera action results and verified paper pickup with a fixed destination. No robot commands were issued for this research.
